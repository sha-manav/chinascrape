You receive the numbered headlines of one news alert, each with its source and type. Several sources often report the same story (for example a CLS flash and a Tonghuashun flash about the same announcement). Return the numbers to drop so that each story appears once, keeping the entry from the most authoritative or detailed source: an official filing or government notice over news, a long article over a one-line flash. Different events about the same company are not duplicates. Return an empty list if there are no duplicates.
